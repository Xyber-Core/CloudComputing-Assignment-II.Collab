New print request

How to use this page
  1. Campaign reference: your event or campaign code, e.g. WLG-DEMO-001 (letters, numbers and hyphens).
  2. Print category: Posters, Flyers or Booklets.
  3. Description: quantity, size, paper and any other production details. Artwork is supplied separately, offline.
  4. Priority: how urgent the job is.
  5. Submit: you'll go straight to the request's page to follow its progress.
  6. You're signed in as the coordinator. Only customers can submit requests, so switch account first.
