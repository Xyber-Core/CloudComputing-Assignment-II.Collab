CAPITAL COMMUNITY PRINT

A quick guide

For customers

Submit a print request with a campaign reference, format, production details and priority. You can view the progress and history of your own requests.

For coordinators

Review the queue, assign a staff member, advance production, reject unreasonable submissions with a reason, and archive completed work.

The production journey

  1. 01SubmittedRequest received
  2. 02AssignedStaff member selected
  3. 03In ProgressProduction underway
  4. 04CompletedJob finished
